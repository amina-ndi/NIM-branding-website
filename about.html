About NIM

Transforming educational institutes through smart automation and digital management

What is NIM?

NIM (Noshahi Institute Manager) is a modern school and institute management software designed to simplify daily administrative tasks and solve common management problems faced by educational institutes.

The system provides an all-in-one digital solution for managing students, staff, classes, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, and reports from a single dashboard. NIM is a complete digital management solution for schools, colleges, and educational institutes that helps institutions operate more efficiently through smart automation and an easy-to-use management system.

Our mission is to empower educational institutions with technology that makes administration effortless, allowing educators to focus on what truly matters - teaching and learning.
