NIM - Noshahi Institute Manager

NIM (Noshahi Institute Manager) is a modern school and institute management software designed to simplify daily administrative tasks and solve common management problems faced by educational institutes. The system provides an all-in-one digital solution for managing students, staff, classes, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, and reports from a single dashboard.

Staff Management

Complete staff administration and HR management system

Add and Manage Staff Records

Easily add new staff members with complete profile information including personal details, qualifications, and employment history.

Assign Salaries Easily

Set up salary structures, allowances, and deductions for each staff member with flexible configuration options.

Manage Staff Profiles and Roles

Assign roles, permissions, and responsibilities to staff members based on their positions and departments.

Student Management

Comprehensive student information and academic tracking system

Add Student Records

Register new students with complete personal information, guardian details, and medical records in one place.

Manage Admissions and Classes

Handle student admissions, class assignments, and section transfers seamlessly throughout the academic year.

Access Student Reports Anytime

Generate and view comprehensive student reports including academic performance, attendance, and behavior records instantly.

Class & Subject Management

Organize Academic Sessions

Fee Management
